1. POWER RAILS & 5V BOOSTER DISTRIBUTION
================================================================================
If using a 5V Step-Up / Booster Module (e.g. MT3608, SX1308, TP4056+Booster):

[ CONNECT TO 5V BOOSTER OUTPUT (STABLE 5V) ]
- MAX98357A Audio Amp  (VIN)      -> Gives maximum 3.2W loud, crystal clear audio
- ESP32-S3 DevKit      (5V / VIN) -> Powers onboard 3.3V LDO for core & Octal PSRAM
- ST7735 1.8" TFT      (VCC)      -> Onboard LDO powers backlight; relieves 3.3V rail
- MicroSD SPI Module   (VCC)      -> For modules with onboard AMS1117-3.3 regulator

[ STRICTLY 3.3V ONLY - CONNECT TO ESP32-S3 3.3V OUT (NEVER CONNECT TO 5V!) ]
- INMP441 MEMS Mic     (VDD)      -> 3.3V ONLY! (5V will permanently burn MEMS mic)
- Left Touch (TTP223)  (VCC)      -> 3.3V ONLY! (5V causes 5V IO OUT which kills GPIO 1)
- Right Touch (TTP223) (VCC)      -> 3.3V ONLY! (5V causes 5V IO OUT which kills GPIO 2)
- Side Touch (TTP223)  (VCC)      -> 3.3V ONLY! (5V causes 5V IO OUT which kills GPIO 3)

[ SYSTEM COMMON GROUND ]
All GND terminals (Booster GND, Battery GND, ESP32-S3 GND, and all modules) 
MUST be tied together into one common ground bus to prevent buzzing and ground loops.

================================================================================
5. CRITICAL HARDWARE RULES & SAFETY PRECAUTIONS
================================================================================
1. STRICT OCTAL PSRAM RESTRICTION (GPIO 33 - 37):
   The ESP32-S3 N16R8 has 8MB Octal PSRAM wired internally to GPIO 33, 34, 35, 
   36, and 37. NEVER connect any external jumper wire to these pins, or the board 
   will immediately kernel panic and fail to boot.

2. SYSTEM COMMON GROUND:
   All modules (TFT, Mic, Amp, Touch Sensors, MicroSD, Booster) MUST share a 
   common ground connection with the ESP32-S3 GND pin.

3. SPEAKER AMPLIFIER WARNING (BTL OUTPUT):
   The MAX98357A uses Bridge-Tied Load (BTL) outputs. 
   NEVER wire SPK- to GND! SPK+ and SPK- must go directly to the speaker's 
   two dedicated terminals.

4. MICROPHONE & TOUCH VOLTAGE:
   The INMP441 MEMS mic and all 3x TTP223 capacitive touch sensors MUST be 
   powered from 3.3V ONLY. If you power TTP223 from 5V, its output will send 
   5V logic into GPIO 1, 2, and 3, permanently burning the ESP32-S3 pins!

================================================================================
6. ON-THE-FLY PIN REMAPPING (ZERO-RECOMPILE CLI)
================================================================================
If you wire any pin to a different GPIO on your breadboard or PCB, you do NOT 
need to recompile the C++ firmware!

Connect the ESP32-S3 to your PC over USB Serial (115200 baud) and run:
    setpin list                 -> View current pin configuration
    setpin <name> <gpio>        -> Remap pin and save to NVS flash
    setpin reset                -> Restore factory default pinout

Examples:
    setpin touch_left 1         -> Remaps Left Ear Touch to GPIO 1
    setpin touch_right 2        -> Remaps Right Ear Touch to GPIO 2
    setpin touch_side 3         -> Remaps Side Flank Touch to GPIO 3
